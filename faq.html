FAQ

Common questions

Basic information about BB369TECH products and services.

Does BB369TECH provide both products and services?

Yes. BB369TECH develops its own digital products and also provides implementation, website, multilingual content, and ongoing support services.

Can a product be configured for one business?

Implementation can include business information, branding, page structure, customer entry points, and staff onboarding.

Which languages are supported?

English and Chinese are commonly supported. Additional languages may be added based on the project.

How do we start?

Send the current website, sample document, or a short description of the business task through the contact page.

What services does BB369TECH offer?

We offer business website design, digital support, and consultations for custom business solutions. View services.

Do I need an existing website?

No. You can ask about a new business website or discuss improvements to an existing one.

Can you update an existing business website?

Website improvements may be available depending on the current website and project requirements. Contact us to review the scope.

What is included in a business website?

Website content and functionality depend on the selected service. Common needs include service pages, business contact information, mobile-friendly presentation and inquiry options. View the current service details.

Do you support Google Business Profile?

Business profile support may be discussed as part of our public-facing digital services. Profile ownership and platform policies remain with the business and the respective provider.

Can you support English and Chinese websites?

Yes, English and Chinese presentation is available for suitable projects. Additional language requirements can be discussed.

Do you guarantee search rankings or new customers?

No. Search visibility and customer inquiries depend on many factors outside any single website or service provider's control.

How are prices and timelines confirmed?

Review the published service information, then confirm the applicable scope, terms, price and schedule before work begins. Contact us for project-specific questions.

Can businesses outside California work with BB369TECH?

Remote digital service requests from elsewhere in the United States may be considered depending on project scope.

Who owns the website and business materials?

Ownership and usage rights depend on the applicable written agreement. Please review the agreed terms before starting a project.

How do I get started?

Choose a service or send an inquiry with a brief description of your business needs. Do not send passwords or sensitive customer records through the public form.
