Planning and scope

What should I prepare before a consultation?

Describe your business, audience and the problem you want to improve, and share an existing website or public business link. Use materials you have rights to share. Initial consultation does not require account passwords or customer lists.

Can you improve my website without rebuilding it?

We can review existing pages, content, domain and functions before agreeing what to preserve and improve. A partial update depends on the existing technology and available access.

When does custom business software make sense?

Consider a custom solution when existing tools do not fit repeated forms, customer follow-up, quotations or collaboration. Describe the real workflow first, then agree the necessary functions.

Can different locations or brands have separate pages?

Separate pages, categories and contact paths can be discussed. Information, access, costs and maintenance need to be agreed for each brand or location; customer data is not assumed to be shared.

Pricing and delivery

Does sending an inquiry authorize a charge?

Sending an inquiry does not accept a quotation or authorize payment. Charges, payment timing and applicable terms should be confirmed before a purchase or work starts.

Are domain, hosting, email and third-party tools included?

Inclusions depend on the selected service and written quotation. Confirm setup costs, renewals, usage charges and account ownership separately; third-party services should not be assumed to be free forever.

How long will delivery take?

Timing depends on scope, materials, complexity and review feedback. Agree milestones, required inputs and acceptance steps before work begins; one timeline does not fit every project.

How are additional features handled?

Agree the scope, cost and schedule of new pages, functions or integrations before implementation. Distinguish included maintenance from new development.

What about refunds, cancellation and renewals?

The terms presented at purchase, selected service and written agreement apply. Confirm whether a purchase is a subscription, its renewal cycle and cancellation process before paying; this FAQ does not replace order terms.

Website and launch

Will the website work on mobile?

Agreed checks should cover text, navigation, images and key actions on mobile, tablet and desktop. Forms and important paths still need acceptance checks; a desktop screenshot alone is insufficient.

Can existing URLs and search content be preserved?

Record existing URLs, business content, titles, canonicals, structured data and links before an upgrade. Preserve valid pages where possible, and agree redirects and regression checks before any necessary path change.

Will launching guarantee search or AI recommendations?

No. Crawlable content, accurate business information and stable links support discovery, but platforms decide whether to index, cite or recommend a business. Rankings, traffic and sales are not guaranteed.

How does a preview differ from a production site?

A preview supports design, content and workflow review; it does not prove every live integration is connected. Verify the deployed version, forms, database, access controls and applicable payment flows before release.

Does adding a product or project require a new homepage?

Usually not. Add verified information and real screenshots to the existing product or project directory while keeping the homepage focused and preserving existing pages and entry points.

Data and inquiries

Where are customer inquiries stored?

Storage and handling depend on the actual service configuration and privacy notice. An existing database does not prove every form or chat is connected; verify the saved record and authorized back-office access.

What if a form says the result could not be confirmed?

Do not repeatedly resubmit or assume either success or complete failure. Contact the published business email with the approximate submission time. Do not send passwords, card information or sensitive customer files.

What does saved with email pending mean?

It means the system reports a saved inquiry while email notification is incomplete. It does not confirm email delivery. The team must check the record and follow the actual workflow; repeated submission should not be used to trigger mail.

Is the CRM public to website visitors?

Internal customer management screens and customer records should not be exposed as public pages. The website explains services and provides contact paths; back-office queries, permissions and access to customer records need separate controls.

What information should I send through a form?

Send only the information requested by that form and that you have permission to share. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or sensitive customer records through a public inquiry form. Agree a secure transfer method if more material is needed.

Support and real project examples

Who maintains the site after launch?

Agree who updates the site, support scope, response arrangements and fees. Self-management or team assistance can be discussed; launch does not automatically include unlimited changes.

Can existing CRM, email or payment systems be connected?

We can assess verified interfaces, permissions, data scope and cost. Integration must be agreed and tested for the project; do not treat it as live before acceptance or put server secrets on public pages.

Are the school pages official school websites?

Pages labeled independent student inquiry or referral are not official school websites. They do not imply an official-site commission or a school endorsement of BB369TECH. Confirm admissions and program matters with the school.

Can I review real projects before contacting you?

Use the project directory to view real pages, business types and documented scope. Page availability does not establish that every inquiry, payment or back-office function has passed acceptance; read the specific project notes.
