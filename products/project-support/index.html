BB369TECH Project Support PWA

A bilingual customer project intake and merchant quotation workspace for service businesses.

Open ApplicationBB369TECH GitHub

What it helps a business do

  • Collect basic customer project information.
  • Import supported supplier quotations and organize item details.
  • Review supplier cost, set customer pricing, and view estimated profit.
  • Prepare professional customer quotations in the merchant's own name.
  • Save, print, or share customer-ready quotation files.

Current modules

Cabinets & Countertops

Quotation preparation for cabinet, accessory, countertop, installation, removal, and delivery items.

Windows & Doors

Supplier quote intake, dimensions, glass and configuration details, customer pricing, and formal quotation output.

Designed for human confirmation

Imported items and prices remain editable. The merchant reviews project details and customer pricing before issuing a quotation.

Customer quotation

The customer-facing file can include merchant branding, contact details, project information, item descriptions, dimensions, quantities, prices, service fees, tax, total, validity date, and terms.
